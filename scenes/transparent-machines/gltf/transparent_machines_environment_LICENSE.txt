A clear sky procedurally generated with scripts for this repository, inspired by the scene's original sky.exr. It is computed from an analytic sky model and contains no photograph, third-party image, or pixel of the original.

Changes: resampled from the PBRT-v4 equal-area map scenes/transparent-machines/source/textures/transparent_machines_sky_equiarea.exr to an equirectangular Radiance HDR in the glTF world orientation (top row +Y, center column +X, three-quarter column +Z), following the scene's PBRT light transform and reflecting world X to match the exported cameras and meshes and rotating with the frame to preserve the authored view with world Y up.
