A dark studio procedurally generated with scripts for this repository: narrow light strips and small bright spots outline the glass and bring out its dispersion. It contains no photograph or third-party image. Use it at intensity 1 in place of the frame's sky.

Aligned to the glTF camera that every view shares, in the glTF world orientation (top row +Y, center column +X, three-quarter column +Z).
