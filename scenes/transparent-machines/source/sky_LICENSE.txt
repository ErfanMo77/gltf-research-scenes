A clear sky procedurally generated with scripts for this repository, inspired
by the scene's original sky.exr, whose redistribution terms could not be
verified. It is computed from an analytic sky model and contains no
photograph, third-party image, or pixel of the original. It has the original's
mean luminance, so the scene's PBRT light scale of 6 applies.
