Transparent Machines

Original Cinema 4D project and models by Mike Winkelmann (Beeple).
Beeple publishes the project files at https://beeple-crap.com/resources
and permits commercial and noncommercial use without required attribution.
His project page identifies the files as Creative Commons without naming a
specific license version.

https://www.behance.net/gallery/11987377/Transparent-Machines
https://vimeo.com/78716671

PBRT frame conversion from mmp/pbrt-v4-scenes, added by Matt Pharr with
credit to Beeple. The upstream README points to Beeple's usage terms.
https://github.com/mmp/pbrt-v4-scenes#transparent-machines

The glTF adaptation uses a separately licensed sky. The original PBRT
sky.exr and upstream render images are linked in provenance.yaml.
